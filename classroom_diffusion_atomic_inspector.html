Scientific Principles of Diffusion

1. What is Diffusion?

Diffusion is the net movement of particles from a region of higher concentration to a region of lower concentration down a concentration gradient until evenly distributed.

2. Thermal Kinetic Energy & Temperature

Particles move because of heat energy. Increasing room temperature causes air and perfume molecules to vibrate and travel faster, accelerating the rate at which students smell the perfume.

3. Molecular Hierarchy

  • Droplet (Macro): Trillions of scent molecules held by intermolecular forces.
  • Particle (Micro Mist): Micro-droplets floating in aerosol suspension.
  • Molecule (Aroma Compound): Chemical structures like Linalool or Isoamyl Acetate ($C_{7}H_{14}O_2$).
  • Atom: Fundamental building blocks (Carbon, Hydrogen, Oxygen) with nuclei and orbiting electrons.
